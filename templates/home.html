About the database

The Terraria Weapons Database is a comprehensive collection of weapons available in the desktop/PC version of Terraria. It includes a wide range of weapons from across the game, with statistics and information updated to date for version 1.4.5.8. There are 581 rows

This database is designed to help players explore the game's weapons, compare different items, and find the information they need to choose which weapons they want to use at different points in the games progression. Whether your a completly new terraria player, or a well seasoned pro, you can definitely find some usefull infomation here.

Searching by type means you can search by the type of the weapon, for example searching "hammer" would give you all hammers, regardless if the word hammer is in their names. The third way you can search, is by all columns. You can use this to find weapons under a specific rarity, damage, or any other colunms
